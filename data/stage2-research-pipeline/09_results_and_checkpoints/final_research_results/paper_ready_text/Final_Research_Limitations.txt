
FINAL RESEARCH LIMITATIONS
==========================

1. Synthetic dataset
--------------------
The Customer Shopping Trends dataset is synthetic. Findings
must therefore not be interpreted as direct evidence about
real customer populations.

2. Structural Gender-Subscription relationship
-----------------------------------------------
The dataset contains a pronounced relationship between Gender
and Subscription Status. Explainability and ablation analyses
demonstrate that predictive performance depends strongly on
this structural characteristic.

3. No causal interpretation
---------------------------
The study is predictive and associative. Neither SHAP,
permutation importance, chi-square analysis, nor ablation
testing establishes causality.

4. Limited dataset size
-----------------------
The dataset contains 3,900 observations, limiting the extent
to which more complex machine-learning methods can be justified.

5. Label scarcity is simulated
------------------------------
The 10%, 20%, and 50% label conditions are experimentally
constructed from a fully labeled dataset. They simulate label
scarcity rather than representing naturally missing labels.

6. Pseudo-label diagnostics use hidden ground truth
---------------------------------------------------
True labels of experimentally hidden samples were used only
for retrospective diagnostic evaluation of pseudo-label
reliability. They were not used for pseudo-label selection or
model training.

7. Clustering was not retained as the primary mechanism
--------------------------------------------------------
K-Prototypes clustering exhibited weak separation. Therefore,
cluster-guided pseudo-labeling was not treated as the primary
method in the final evidence.

8. Single dataset
-----------------
External validation on additional real-world datasets is
required before generalizing the findings.
