Scientific interpretation
-------------------------

Multiple independent analyses converge on the conclusion that
Gender is the dominant predictive feature for Subscription
Status in this dataset.

Grouped permutation importance and SHAP both rank Gender first.
The raw-data structural analysis shows a pronounced association
between Gender and Subscription Status, while the post-hoc
Gender-ablation experiment demonstrates a large reduction in
predictive performance when Gender is unavailable.

These results indicate predictive dependence and dataset-level
association. They do NOT establish causation.

The analyzed Customer Shopping Trends dataset is synthetic.
Therefore, the observed Gender-Subscription relationship should
be interpreted as a structural property of this dataset rather
than evidence of a generalizable real-world behavioral effect.
